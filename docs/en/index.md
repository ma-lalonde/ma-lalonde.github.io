## How we work together

1. **Discovery** - 30 minutes, free. You walk me through a normal week and what gets redone by hand.
2. **Feedback** - written, free. What is worth doing, the work it removes, and roughly what it takes.
3. **Feasibility, diagnostic and planning** - a specification and a test plan in plain language, and they are yours.
4. **Implementation** - in iterations, tested by me and approved by you, step by step.
5. **Support** - updates, backups, vulnerability management, continuous integration and delivery.
