# Grants

Several programs can pay part of your project, including my fees. I tell you which one targets your project; the administrator decides.

## Which programs might cover your project?

Every program is listed below. Answer the questions to rule out the programs whose size, industry, territory or project-type criteria do not match your business; ruled-out programs are folded at the bottom with the reason. Each program is a grant, a loan you repay or a tax credit, and the tool says which. Amounts and conditions change, so check them with the administrator before you commit.

## Order matters

The application is filed before anything is signed or spent. Most programs refuse expenses made before the application is filed, and some count a signed contract as an expense.

The order to follow: my proposal, your application to the administrator, its approval, then the contract, then the work.

None of these programs is guaranteed to you: I tell you which one seems to target your project, and the administrator decides. To discuss it, [tell me about your business](/en/contact).
