# Grants

Several programs can pay part of my fees. I tell you which one targets your project; the administrator decides.

Your business might be eligible for one or more of the programs below. Each row links to the page of the body that administers it: amounts and conditions change, so check them with that body before you commit.

| Program | What it pays | Who it is for | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ESSOR, stream 1B](https://www.investquebec.com/fr/financement/programmes-gouvernementaux/essor/appui-la-concretisation-de-projets-dinvestissement) (Investissement Québec) | A grant of 50% of external consultant fees for a digital diagnostic, a digital plan and system selection, up to $20,000 per company for the life of the program | Quebec company with 250 employees or fewer and revenue of $2.5M or more, with a French-language website | The program ends on March 31, 2027, and intake can be suspended without notice. Some sectors are no longer prioritized since March 13, 2026 |
| [ESSOR, stream 1C](https://www.investquebec.com/fr/financement/programmes-gouvernementaux/essor/appui-la-concretisation-de-projets-dinvestissement) (Investissement Québec) | A grant of 50% of consultant fees to implement the digital plan, up to $50,000 per company for the life of the program. Equipment installation and capital expenses are not covered | Same conditions as stream 1B, and a digital plan from a stream 1B completed in the last 24 months | The plan can have been made by someone other than me. Cannot be combined with another Ministry of Economy program on the same project |
| [Desjardins Fonds C](https://www.desjardins.com/fr/a-propos/communaute/developpement-collectif/fonds-c.html) | Non-repayable aid of up to $20,000 (25% of the project, from $5,000 to $20,000, according to the application form), for projects such as digital transformation or cybersecurity | Desjardins member (business current account holder; you can become one when you apply), in commercial-industrial, agricultural, agri-food or tech sectors. Not real estate | A caisse committee decides. No call dates: the application goes through your advisor |
| [Ville de Québec, consulting vouchers](https://www.ville.quebec.qc.ca/gens_affaires/financement/productivite/bons-accompagnement.aspx) | A non-repayable contribution of 30% of eligible expenses, up to $30,000 per company per year, for professional fees, consulting services and studies | Incorporated company with its head office and project in the Quebec City agglomeration or Wendake, in an eligible sector (for example manufacturing and industry 4.0, agri-food) | The City must approve my mandate before the contract is signed. Expenses made before a complete application do not count |
| [PME MTL, Innovation, Productivity and Sustainable Development Fund](https://pmemtl.com/services/fonds-innovation-productivite-developpement-durable) | A grant of up to $50,000, not exceeding 80% of the project cost, to improve productivity (equipment, robotics, automation) or launch an innovation | Incorporated company with an establishment in Montreal and its head office in Quebec, putting in 20% of the project cost; business plan, two-year forecasts and a sustainable development approach | The budget is limited and the amount varies by territory. Whether consultant fees are eligible is to be confirmed with PME MTL |
| [MFOR, employer stream](https://www.quebec.ca/entreprises-et-travailleurs-autonomes/administrer-gerer/embauche-gestion-personnel/former-main-oeuvre/developper-competences) (Ministère de l'Emploi et de la Solidarité sociale) | Training of your employees only: generally up to 50% of eligible expenses, and 75% for training tied to new technology or information technology | Quebec employer with a NEQ, priority to small businesses of 6 to 99 employees; training in French | Does not pay for building or deploying a system. Common office and accounting software are not new technologies. The trainer must be approved by the Ministry |

## Order matters

The application is filed before anything is signed or spent. Most programs refuse expenses made before the application is filed, and some count a signed contract as an expense.

The order to follow: my proposal, your application to the administrator, its approval, then the contract, then the work.

None of these programs is guaranteed to you: I tell you which one seems to target your project, and the administrator decides. To discuss it, [tell me about your business](/en/contact).
