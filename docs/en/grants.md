## Order matters

The application is filed before anything is signed or spent. Most programs refuse expenses made before the application is filed, and some count a signed contract as an expense.

The order to follow: my proposal, your application to the administrator, its approval, then the contract, then the work.

None of these programs is guaranteed to you: I tell you which one seems to target your project, and the administrator decides. To discuss it, [tell me about your business](/en/contact).
