# Offers

Everything starts with a free discovery meeting and written feedback. Then you choose what fits the size of your project.

## Pay as you go {#a-la-carte}

For small projects: $150 an hour, billed in 30-minute increments. You pay for what is done, with no quote.

## Custom projects {#custom}

For larger projects: a diagnostic, a plan, then the implementation. The price is set per project, after the free discovery. Your project [may qualify for grants](/en/grants).

## AI, safely {#ai}

Use AI without exposing your business. I protect your data from loss and from unauthorized access, and your customers' data from leaking. I also build full applications, with their test bench, using AI. [Catena](https://catena.run) is the public example.

## Maintenance {#maintenance}

Two ways to go:

- **One price** - I deliver the system, it runs daily and you operate it.
- **Monthly subscription** - I keep it running, fixes included. Prepaid for 1 year, then pay as you go or monthly.

## Examples of automations delivered {#examples}

- Manual data entry from a paper document, a spreadsheet or one piece of software into another
- Connected instruments, with data collected automatically and gathered in an intuitive dashboard
- Automatic monitoring and control of a climate or a machine
- Connecting files and databases previously isolated in silos, to make them accessible and to increase the speed, accuracy and efficiency of processes
- Automatic and secure backups, updates and deployments
- Data anonymization, separation of steps and access management, to improve efficiency with AI without risking the loss or theft of data
