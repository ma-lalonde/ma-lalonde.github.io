For larger projects: a diagnostic, a plan, then the implementation. The price is set per project, after the free discovery. Your project [may qualify for grants](/en/grants).

## AI, safely {#ai}

Use AI without exposing your business. I protect your data from loss and from unauthorized access, and your customers' data from leaking. I also build full applications, with their test bench, using AI. [Catena](https://catena.run) is the public example.

## Examples of automations delivered {#examples}

Here are seven situations I often meet. Each one describes what you live in your week, what I do, and examples delivered for clients or for my own businesses. Clients are not named.

### Data entered by hand {#data-entry}

**In your week** - The same numbers are retyped into the spreadsheet, the accounting software and the website.

**What I do** - I connect your software so the data is entered once.

**Examples**

- My own food manufacturing business: online store, payments, accounting and inventory synced. At least 1 hour a week recovered, and one painful task gone.
- An online community: member data synced automatically between the social platform and the CRM.

### Reading measurements and writing them down {#measurements}

**In your week** - Someone reads a gauge or a probe, then writes the result down by hand.

**What I do** - I instrument the process: measurements are taken, recorded and monitored without anyone stepping in.

**Examples**

- A mushroom production greenhouse: humidity and CO2 are measured, controlled and monitored by a system I designed and deployed.
- My own food manufacturing business: roasting, which was watched by hand, automated in a closed loop.

### Manual copying from one application to another {#copying}

**In your week** - You export from one piece of software to import into another, or you change platform and your data has to follow.

**What I do** - I migrate the data and sync the applications so the manual copying goes away.

**Examples**

- About 30,000 members migrated from one community platform to another, with the social platform and the CRM synced.
- Badge distribution and progress saving across fitness programs, automated.

### Manually controlling a process {#process-control}

**In your week** - A machine needs watching and its settings adjusted, and quality varies from one batch to the next.

**What I do** - I design a closed-loop control, with the electronics and software that go with it.

**Examples**

- Roasting automated in a closed loop: 3 to 5 hours a week recovered, and more consistent quality.
- Temperature control on the tempering machines: hours a week of redone batches eliminated, and better quality.

### Siloed data, unusable for improving processes {#silos}

**In your week** - Data is scattered across software, spreadsheets and notebooks: you cannot see what works and what does not.

**What I do** - I bring the data together in one place and make your machines visible in a clear dashboard.

**Examples**

- My own food manufacturing business: store, payments, accounting and inventory brought together in one management software, which saves time at tax season and when ordering ingredients.
- Every machine modified and controlled remotely from a dashboard, which made it possible to improve recipes quickly.

### Deployments, updates and backups done by hand {#deployments}

**In your week** - Updating the website or a piece of software takes someone technical, and nobody has checked that the backup restores.

**What I do** - I set up automatic deployments, updates and backups, with restores that are exercised.

**Examples**

- A website and a video compression application that deploy and update without technical knowledge, with access to IT resources configured and secured.
- [Catena](https://catena.run): a blank server becomes a running suite of business applications, with authentication, backups, monitoring and restore built in from the start.

### Tasks AI can do, without exposing your data {#ai-data}

**In your week** - Repetitive reading, sorting or writing tasks could be handed to AI, but you do not want your data or your customers' data to leave your hands.

**What I do** - I work out which tasks AI can do, then set them up so your data is protected from loss, unauthorized access and leaks.

**Examples**

- [Catena](https://catena.run): a full application and its automated test bench, built with AI.
