# Work with me

Small businesses run on work that gets redone by hand every week: retyping an order into a second system, chasing an invoice, rebuilding the same report, checking whether the backup ran. I take that work away, on software you own, and I write down what it must do before I build it.

## How we work together

1. **Discovery call** - 30 minutes, free. You walk me through a normal week. I ask what gets redone by hand, what gets copied between two screens, and what breaks when someone is away.
2. **What I can help with** - written, free. A list. For each item: the work it removes, roughly what it takes to build, and whether it is worth doing at all. Some items say no. You owe me nothing at this stage.
3. **Audit and plan** - the deliverable is a written specification and a test plan, in plain language. They are yours, and they are complete enough for someone else to build from if you would rather.
4. **Build** - in iterations, against the test plan. Each milestone ends with the tests running in front of you.
5. **Run** - monthly, optional, cancellable. Backups verified, updates applied, restores drilled on a schedule. Or a handoff, the documentation, and I am done.

## Data, privacy, and compliance {#data}

Automating a process means moving personal information around. In Quebec that triggers specific obligations, and most of them are not technical:

- A named, published person responsible for personal information.
- Why each piece of data is held, for how long, and its destruction at expiry.
- Access, correction, deletion and portability requests: who answers, how fast, in what format.
- An incident register and a notification procedure that runs on a clock, not on a log file.
- A privacy impact assessment when a system is acquired or rebuilt, and when personal information leaves Quebec.
- An assessment of the vendors your data passes through.
- The loop that keeps it all true: risk register, review, corrective action.

I am not a lawyer and I do not certify anyone. What I deliver is the implementation and the written procedures: the register, the privacy impact assessment, the breach decision tree, the per-application data export matrix, and evidence that the backups actually restore.
