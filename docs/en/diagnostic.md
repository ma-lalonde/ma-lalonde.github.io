# ESSOR digital diagnostic

An ESSOR digital diagnostic (stream 1B) examines how your business works today and ends in a clear digital plan. Your business might get part of its fees reimbursed: [the programs in detail](/en/grants).

## What you get

The deliverables the program prescribes:

- At least 9 business processes reviewed, 3 of them with problems.
- A digital maturity index for the business.
- An interim report and a digital plan.
- The priority projects.
- For each project, a needs grid and specifications.
- An analysis of the candidate solutions and the choice among them.
- A change-management plan.
- A final report.

## Who it is for

A Quebec company that has:

- 250 employees or fewer;
- revenue of $2.5M or more;
- a French-language website;
- two years of financial statements;
- a strategic plan.

Some sectors are not prioritized by the program: [see the detail](/en/grants).

## What it costs

- Preparing the grant application costs $500. This work comes before the application is filed, so the grant does not cover it.
- The diagnostic is billed at $150 an hour. A complete ESSOR diagnostic usually takes 100 to 150 hours, so $15,000 to $22,500.
- ESSOR could reimburse 50% of that amount, up to $20,000 per company: $7,500 to $11,250 for a diagnostic of this size. You pay me by stage, and Investissement Québec could reimburse your company in at most two payments, the last one on the final report.

None of this is guaranteed: Investissement Québec decides.

## The steps

1. My written proposal.
2. The application. I prepare it for $500; your company files it with its ClicSÉQUR account, the ADN 4.0 self-diagnostic report, its financial statements and its strategic plan.
3. Investissement Québec's decision: about 2 to 3 months.
4. The contract.
5. The diagnostic.
6. Afterwards, ESSOR stream 1C could pay 50% of carrying out the plan, up to $50,000, within 24 months.

Nothing is signed or spent before the application is filed: [why order matters](/en/grants).

## Deadline

ESSOR can authorize aid until March 31, 2027. Given the processing time, file the application by the end of 2026 or early 2027.
