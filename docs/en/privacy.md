# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what personal information this site collects, why, where it is kept and how to exercise your rights under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector.

## Person in charge

Marc-Antoine Lalonde is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. For any question or request: <EmailLink user="marc" domain="ma-lalonde.dev" />.

## What I collect

| When | Information | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You write to me | Name, email, message | Answer your request | My email, hosted in Germany (mailbox.org) |
| You book a call | Name, email, chosen time, notes you add | Schedule the call | My Nextcloud calendar, hosted in Quebec (Montreal or Beauharnois) |
| You received an email from me | Name and title if your website publishes them, business email, company name and activity | Offer you my services; this information comes from your company's website and the Quebec enterprise register | My prospecting database and my ERPNext, hosted in Quebec (Montreal or Beauharnois) |
| You browse the site | IP address, requested page, in server logs | Serve pages and keep the site secure | GitHub Pages (United States) |
| You browse the site | Page visited, referring site, browser, system, device type, language, country, region and city (from the IP address, which is not kept) | Count visits | Umami, on my own server hosted in Quebec |

The site sets no tracking cookies. Visit counting (Umami) uses no cookie and does not keep your IP address; if your browser asks not to be tracked, nothing is counted. The site keeps your light or dark mode choice in your browser. The Nextcloud booking page, on its own domain, may set technical cookies it needs to work.

## Transfers outside Quebec

Cloudflare and GitHub are companies based in the United States. Information they process for this site may be stored outside Quebec. Visit-counting requests also pass through Cloudflare before they reach my server.

My email is hosted in Germany by mailbox.org: the emails you send me or I send you are kept there, outside Quebec.

## Retention

- Messages and bookings: 2 years after our last exchange, then deleted.
- Prospecting information: 2 years after the last exchange, then deleted. If you ask to stop receiving my emails, I keep only your address, on a do-not-contact list, so I never write to you again.
- Visit statistics: 2 years.
- Technical logs: according to GitHub's and Cloudflare's policies.

## Stop receiving my emails

Reply "stop" to any of my emails. Your address is then added to the do-not-contact list and I never write to it again.

## Who has access

Only me, and the providers named above for the purposes described. I do not sell or rent your information and do not use it for advertising.

## Your rights

You can ask to access your information, have it corrected, withdraw your consent or receive a copy in a structured, commonly used technological format. Write to the address above: I answer within 30 days.

If the answer does not satisfy you, you can file a complaint with the [Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca).
